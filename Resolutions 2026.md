# Biggest regret from 2025

There are a couple of things I wish I've done differently last year, like to prepare before the time I was let go from the company or the decaying of habits such as going to the gym as I felt unworthy due to being unemployed.

Beliefs are a huge part of how I've been behaving from the time when I starting to have those thoughts of hatred against me, to never have a time for validating myself by celebrating any kind of achievements. That's the biggest regret I have.

Now that I think of it, it IS the root for every bad decision or ingrained habit I've picked since my younghood.

From now on **I will think highly of myself**. **I've been putting the effort, and I deserve it**. No more neglect, or non-sense actions of self-hatred.

# Premortem

Completely related to the biggest regret, I will let go of those emotions against myself, now that I'm crystal clear of what they've done to me during the last decade or more.

Every time I ever get the time to think about myself and what I've achieved so far it'll be thoughts of compassion and happiness of how far I've made it.

# One Word Theme

## Discipline


# First hour in the day is sacred

I'll start my days either going to the gym or meditating. There won't be a chance for distractions to avoid winning a whole meaningful day.

>[!NOTE]
>This does not mean to discard leisure time but to prioritize other tasks during the day.


# 85% Rule

Trial and error any kind of knowledge I'd like to explore and really put it into practice. Do it in a way that 9/10 times I'm able to achieve a functional proof of that knowledge.

If it's too easy I wouldn't be learning something that challenges me.


# Less friction facilitates focus

Try to order your room and surroundings so that it is easier to maintain laser focus interactions with courses or just to learn without easy distractions due to a polluted environment.

# Divide the year as 4 chapters

90 day periods are easier to track than 365 days.

For each period do a check on what have been the improvements, challenges, achievements, etc.

# Don't overlook daily wins

Make sure to write down on what has been achieved daily and to dial on those on a weekly basis.
